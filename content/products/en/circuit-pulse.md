Every robot in our lineup — Atlas Arm's vision-guided placement, Rex's thermal inspection, Forge Line's real-time tracking — runs on the same board. Circuit Pulse is that board, sold on its own for teams building their own hardware around it.

## Fast enough to matter, small enough to hide

40 TOPS of inference at under 15 watts means Circuit Pulse fits inside a robot's existing power and thermal budget instead of forcing a redesign around it. Vision and sensor-fusion models run fully on-device — no network dependency, no latency spike when a connection drops.

## One board, any robot

The same mounting pattern and connector layout ships across our entire catalog, so a Circuit Pulse bought today works as a drop-in upgrade for a robot bought two years ago. Full SDK and model-conversion tools are included, with reference builds for the most common vision and inspection tasks.
