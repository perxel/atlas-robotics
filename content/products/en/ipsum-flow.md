Most teams lose hours a week to the same five-step process: someone fills out a form, someone else approves it, a third person gets notified, and a spreadsheet somewhere gets updated by hand. **Ipsum Flow** turns that whole chain into a rule you set up once.

## How it works

Build a flow visually — trigger, conditions, actions — and Ipsum Flow runs it every time, exactly the same way, without anyone remembering to do the next step.

- Start from a template or build from scratch
- Connect the tools you already use: email, forms, spreadsheets, and 40+ other apps
- Get a full history of who did what and when, automatically

## Who it's for

Operations and support teams that are still coordinating handoffs over email or chat, and want the process to run itself instead of relying on someone remembering.
