Nova Grip came out of a simple complaint from early users of single-motor prosthetics: gripping a coffee cup and gripping a pen shouldn't feel like the same motion. Every finger has its own actuator, so the hand can hold either shape on purpose, not as a compromise.

## Built on our industrial actuator work

The same force-sensing joints that let our Atlas Arm stop on unexpected contact are scaled down here — Nova Grip senses when an object starts to slip and tightens automatically, without the wearer having to think about it.

## Fitted, not just sized

Grip patterns are tuned per wearer during fitting, and adjustable afterward through a companion app — a firmer pinch for typing, a fuller wrap for lifting, saved as presets switched with a double-tap gesture.
