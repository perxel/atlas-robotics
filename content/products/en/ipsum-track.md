Automation is only trustworthy if you can see it working. **Ipsum Track** turns every step Ipsum Flow runs into a live event you can watch, search, and get alerted on.

## How it works

Every trigger, action, and handoff streams into one timeline, grouped by whatever matters to you — a customer, an order, a support case.

- A live feed of every automated step across every connected tool
- Configurable alerts the moment something stalls or errors out
- One shareable timeline per record, not scattered across five apps

## Who it's for

Operations teams running enough automation that "did this actually happen?" has become a real question — and want the answer in seconds, not a support ticket.
