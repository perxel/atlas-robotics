Most chat apps end up as a second copy of everything — the doc lives one place, the conversation about it lives somewhere else. **Ipsum Chat** keeps the two attached.

## How it works

Every channel can link to a project or document, so context is one click away instead of a scavenger hunt through old messages.

- Threaded channels stay tied to the project or doc they're about
- Start a huddle with screen share directly from any channel
- Full-text search across every channel and thread, forever

## Who it's for

Teams who've outgrown scattered DMs and want conversations to stay attached to the work they're actually about.
