A board that just tracks status still needs someone to notice a card moved and do something about it. **Ipsum Boards** closes that gap by connecting directly to Ipsum Flow.

## How it works

Build a board for any process, then attach automations to any column change — moving a card can notify a team, update a record, or start a whole new flow.

- Drag-and-drop boards for sprints, pipelines, or approvals
- Column changes can trigger an Ipsum Flow automation directly
- Shared, real-time views across every team on the account

## Who it's for

Teams running work through boards today who are tired of manually doing the thing a card move should have triggered automatically.
