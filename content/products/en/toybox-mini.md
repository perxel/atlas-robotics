Toybox Mini started as an engineering joke — "what if Atlas Arm's force sensors, but a toy" — and ended up being one of the most requested products in our lineup. It's the same safety philosophy as our industrial robots, scaled down to something a five-year-old can hug.

## Safe by the same standards as the factory floor

Force-limited joints and a soft-touch shell mean Toybox Mini stops the instant it meets unexpected resistance — a small hand included. It's the identical sensing approach used in Atlas Arm, tuned for a completely different kind of workplace.

## Grows with a kid, not just a battery

Toybox Mini learns names, favorite games, and even a child's voice over weeks of use, adjusting its games and responses as it goes. Six hours of runtime and a wireless charging dock mean it's rarely the one asking to take a break.
