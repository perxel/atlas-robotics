A broken bracket used to mean a purchase order, a shipping wait, and a line sitting idle in between. FormPrint 3D exists to collapse that gap: design the part, queue the print, and have it in hand before the next shift starts.

## Built for parts that carry load

Engineering-grade nylon and carbon-fiber composite filaments mean FormPrint 3D isn't limited to jigs and prototypes — it prints brackets, mounts, and fixtures rated for real mechanical load, the same category of part it's usually standing in for.

## Plugged into the line, not next to it

FormPrint 3D reads print requests directly from a facility's maintenance system, so a technician can queue a replacement part from the same ticket that flagged the failure — no separate software, no manual file handoff.
