A form that just emails you a submission is only half done. **Ipsum Forms** connects directly to Ipsum Flow, so a submission is the first step of a process, not the last.

## How it works

Build a form visually, then attach it to a flow. Every field becomes a trigger condition, so a support request and a sales lead can route to completely different automations from the same builder.

- Conditional logic shows or hides fields based on earlier answers
- Submissions land straight in the flow that should handle them
- File uploads and spam filtering are on by default, no setup required

## Who it's for

Teams tired of forms that just generate an email nobody owns — and want the next step to happen automatically instead of waiting for someone to notice.
