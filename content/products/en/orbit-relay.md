Rex and Forge Line units are built to keep working when a facility's own network doesn't — but that only matters if the connection above them holds too. Orbit Relay is the layer we built to make that promise real for fleets operating across large or disconnected sites.

## A relay, not a router

Orbit Relay satellites mesh with each other and with any registered ground unit in range, so a Rex patrol robot in a basement corridor and a Forge Line cell three buildings over stay on the same network without either one depending on site Wi-Fi.

## Built to last a deployment cycle

Solar-electric propulsion keeps each satellite station-keeping for a rated seven years — long enough to outlast most of the hardware fleets it's supporting. Capacity is sold by coverage region and data tier, not by satellite; talk to our sales team about what a deployment actually needs.
