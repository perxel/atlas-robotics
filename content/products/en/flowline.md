Production layouts change more often than most conveyor systems can keep up with. FlowLine's sections click together and reroute through software, so a layout change that used to mean a weekend of rewiring now takes an afternoon.

## Routes by sight, not stickers

Instead of relying on barcodes that can be scratched, misapplied, or missing, FlowLine's vision system identifies and routes parts by shape and appearance — which also means it catches a misrouted or defective part before it reaches the next station.

## Plays well with the rest of the floor

FlowLine ships pre-configured to feed Atlas Arm and Forge Line work cells directly, and exposes an open API for anything else already running on a line. Most installs are up and sorting on day one, with layout adjustments handled entirely in software after that.
