An approval that lives in someone's inbox is an approval that gets forgotten. **Ipsum Approvals** turns a sign-off chain into a rule, with reminders that do the chasing for you.

## How it works

Set up who needs to approve, in what order, and Ipsum Approvals routes the request and follows up automatically until it is resolved.

- Chain approvers in sequence, or run them in parallel
- Automatic reminders escalate until someone takes action
- A complete, timestamped sign-off history on every request

## Who it's for

Teams where an approval regularly stalls in someone's inbox for days, and want the process to nudge itself along instead.
