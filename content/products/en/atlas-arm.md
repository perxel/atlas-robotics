Most robotic arms are either precise or fast — Atlas Arm was built to stop making teams choose. It holds sub-millimeter repeatability at full speed, across a working envelope big enough to cover an entire assembly cell without repositioning the base.

## Built for the whole shift

Atlas Arm runs on a duty cycle rated for three shifts a day, seven days a week, with predictive maintenance alerts that flag a worn joint weeks before it would cause downtime — not after a line stops.

## Swap tools, not robots

The quick-change head accepts any gripper in our catalog, from vacuum cups to parallel jaws to the same force-sensing fingers we use in Nova Grip. One arm, reconfigured in seconds, covers packaging, welding, and inspection work that used to need three separate machines.

Every Atlas Arm ships with a six-month onboarding program: our engineers program the first three routines with your team, then hand off the rest.
