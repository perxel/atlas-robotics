New hires at fast-growing teams spend their first weeks just figuring out where things live. **Ipsum Sync** gives everyone one shared workspace instead of a scavenger hunt across six different apps.

## What it replaces

- The Slack thread nobody can find again
- The doc that only one person knows the link to
- The status-update meeting that exists because nobody trusts the tracker

## How teams use it

Projects, docs, and conversations live in one place, synced two-way with the calendar and file tools you already use. Invite clients or contractors as guests without buying them a full seat.
