Today we're launching [Ipsum Insights](/products/ipsum-insights), our answer to a question a lot of customers asked us after using Ipsum Flow: "now that the process runs itself, how do we actually see what it's doing?"

## What's in this release

- **Pre-built dashboards** for sales, support, and product usage, ready the moment you connect a data source
- **Custom charts** for anything else you track, built without writing a query
- **Scheduled reports** delivered to Slack or email on whatever cadence your team actually reads them

## Why we built it this way

Most reporting tools ask you to remember to check them. We wanted the opposite: a dashboard that's always current, so "current" stops being a question anyone has to ask.

## Try it

Ipsum Insights is available today on every plan that includes Ipsum Flow, with a standalone plan starting at $49/mo. [See what's included](/products/ipsum-insights).
